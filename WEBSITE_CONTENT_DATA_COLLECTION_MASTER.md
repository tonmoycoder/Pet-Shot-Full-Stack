## 4. BANGLADESH WILDLIFE CHECK

The current Wildlife (Conservation and Security) Act, 2026 says wildlife possession, purchase, sale, transport or keeping can require licence, possession certificate or permit depending on the case. The Forest Department also publishes pet-bird import/NOC/licensing information.

Therefore, potentially wild/native birds such as ঘুঘু or বাবুই must NOT automatically be listed as for-sale inventory.

For each potentially regulated bird:
1. identify scientific name;
2. determine legal/wildlife status;
3. check whether licence/permit/possession evidence is relevant;
4. flag for human verification;
5. publish only after confirmation.

## 14. LICENSE NOTES

Unsplash: free commercial/non-commercial use under its current license; attribution not required, but appreciated.
Pexels: free commercial use under its current license; additional rights can apply to people/brands/trademarks.
Pixabay: free use under its license, but verify additional rights/trademarks/privacy.
Burst: free commercial/non-commercial use under its license; current library remains available.
Openverse: openly licensed/public-domain discovery; verify the license of each asset.
Wikimedia Commons: license varies file-by-file; verify each file and provide required attribution/share-alike.

## 18. RESEARCH AGENT WORKFLOW

1. Inspect existing CMS and content folders.
2. Research candidate species/products.
3. Record sources.
4. Separate research facts from shop inventory.
5. Flag legal/wildlife items.
6. Build candidate datasets.
7. Ask for shop confirmation where needed.
8. Only then publish production CMS content.

## 19. QUALITY GATE

A record may be published only when:
- name/category is verified;
- availability is verified;
- price is verified or intentionally hidden;
- media license/source is recorded;
- legal review is complete where relevant;
- health/medicine claims are reviewed;
- last verification date exists.

Otherwise use draft/needs-verification.
